Riverside, early morning

Editor’s note

Phnom Penh does not reveal itself from a list. It takes a wrong turn down the right street, a shopfront that hasn’t changed its sign in a decade, a room that only opens when someone remembers to unlock it. Nothing in this guide is trending — everything was visited, considered, and chosen by hand.

Field note — 01

The Riverside, before the heat arrives

For a few hours after sunrise, the strip along the Tonle Sap belongs to noodle carts, tai chi, and the low light coming off the water. We keep returning to three addresses along this stretch — a print shop, a coffee counter, and a bar that doesn’t open a menu until dusk.

Field note — 02

Tuol Tompoung’s back streets

Behind the Russian Market’s stalls sits a quieter district of workshops — tailors, weavers, and record shops sharing tin-roofed buildings that rarely bother with signage. Ask, and someone will point the way.
